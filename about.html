About Fieldnotes

We are a two-person studio that started in 2021 after building a website for a neighbourhood bakery. Word spread, and we have been making websites for small teams ever since.

How we work

We keep projects small, communicate in plain language and share progress every week. You always know what is done, what is next and what it costs.

What we value

Accessibility, speed and simplicity. A good website should be easy to read, quick to load and simple to update.
